Request to join.

Club Pretentious is selective by design. This page summarizes expectations (as previously communicated in formal invitations).

Standards
Briefly, since brevity is confidence

We do not recruit; we occasionally acknowledge. Membership is considered to protect the atmosphere.

Conversation is pursued with care. Discretion is a virtue. Taste is a responsibility. Effort is assumed.

Dress is classical Western fashion for all—tailored, traditional, and not fast fashion. Good fit matters. Loud branding does not.

If you accept these premises without argument, you are already communicating well.
How to request
One email. Measured tone.

Send your request to contact@akbarq.com. Include your name, why you believe you belong in a room where standards are enforced, and a sentence demonstrating control.

We recommend the subject line: Request — Club Pretentious.

Replies are not immediate. That is intentional.
