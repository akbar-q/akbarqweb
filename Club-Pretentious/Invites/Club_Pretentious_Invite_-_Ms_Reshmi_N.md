# Club Pretentious
**Office of the President**

1 February 2026

Ms Reshmi N.

**Subject: Invitation to Club Pretentious (by deliberate and singular exception)**

Madam,

It is not our habit to *invite* so much as to *acknowledge*—and yet, on this occasion, we permit ourselves a rare gesture of inclusion.

You are hereby extended an invitation to attend **Club Pretentious**, an institution of cultivated restraint and ceremonial elegance, where discretion is a virtue, taste is a responsibility, and effort is assumed.

Club Pretentious exists for those who understand that refinement is not an accessory; it is an operating system. Our gatherings are not “events.” They are **occasions**. Conversation is pursued with care, wit is deployed with precision, and one arrives on time as though punctuality were a family crest.

**Membership & Conduct (briefly, since brevity is a form of confidence)**

- **Exclusivity**: Admission is selective, not to flatter ourselves (though it does), but to protect the atmosphere.
- **Comportment**: We prefer the unhurried cadence of people who have read at least one book for no practical reason.
- **Presentation**: We dress as though the evening has been anticipated.

**Dress Code (observed, not debated)**

Our dress code is simply **classical Western fashion**—for both men and women—executed with restraint, good fit, and a certain quiet seriousness.

In brief: **tailored, traditional, and not fast fashion**.

Examples of appropriate attire include (non-exhaustive):

- Well-cut **suits**, **sport coats**, blazers, waistcoats, and structured outerwear (e.g., wool overcoats, trench coats).
- **Classic shirts** and blouses; ties, scarves, and tasteful accessories are welcomed when chosen with intent.
- **Tailored trousers**, skirts, and dresses in traditional silhouettes.
- **Proper footwear** (leather oxfords, loafers, brogues, boots, or equally formal equivalents).

We do not “ban” casual wear; we simply note that it would be an earnest misunderstanding.

**An Invitation, Not a Campaign**

Should you accept, your presence will be regarded as a compliment to the club, and your absence will be regarded as evidence of self-control—either is respectable.

To confirm attendance, reply with a brief acceptance. Excess enthusiasm is not required.

With measured anticipation,

**Akbar Qamar**  
President, Club Pretentious
