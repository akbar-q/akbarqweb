Manifesto
A brief statement of standards

Club Pretentious is not a hobby; it is an atmosphere. We convene for occasions rather than events, and we arrive as though punctuality were an inherited virtue.

We practice restraint: in volume, in posture, in the number of exclamation marks we allow into polite correspondence. We favor classical Western tailoring—proper fit, honest materials, and the kind of elegance that does not require a logo.

Conversation is undertaken with care. Taste is treated as a responsibility. And if something must be done, it is done quietly, correctly, and without announcement.

Admission is selective by design, for atmosphere is fragile.
