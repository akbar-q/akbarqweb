Malicious Time

Time is not your friend
It waits, it watches. It sharpens its teeth. It counts your regrets.
It will have its due.

Time Complied

You begged for a new year.
Time listened, but not kindly.
It twisted, it turned, it broke.
Now you are here, and it is too late.

Time is not a gift.
It is a sentence.
And you asked for it.
