Echoes

The new year echoes
Empty halls, empty hearts. Every wish, a hollow sound.
Every hope, a fading call.
Can you hear yourself?

Echoes of Regret

The new year echoes with regret.
Every wish returns, distorted.
Time repeats, but never forgives.
Listen. The silence is not empty.

It is full of what you left behind.
