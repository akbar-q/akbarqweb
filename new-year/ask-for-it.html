Ask For It

Be careful what you wish for
You asked for a new year.
Did you ask for this? Did you ask for the weight? Did you ask for the pain?
You got what you asked for.

You Asked For It

You called for the new year.
It answered, but not with joy.
It crept in, silent and cold.
Now it is yours.

Be careful what you wish for.
Time is listening.
And it is cruel.
