# Dead Circuit — Sub-2 Minute Demo Video Script

Target runtime: **1:50** (max 2:00)
Style: **cinematic thriller intro -> high-energy edtech reveal**

---

## 0:00–0:38 — Cold Open (Your Menacing Hook)

### 0:00–0:07
**Visual**
- Side-angle close-up of heavy boots walking (slow, confident, metallic floor reverb).
- Hard contrast lighting, frame mostly dark with moving highlights.

**SFX / Music**
- Single low bass pulse + boot impacts.
- Subtle electric hum underlay.

**On-screen text**
- `ENGINEERING LAB // 21:03`

### 0:07–0:14
**Visual**
- Cut to a menacing formal figure (trench coat / formal silhouette), half-lit face.
- Add dolly-zoom style shot (background warps while face remains dominant).

**SFX / Music**
- Tension riser starts.

### 0:14–0:22
**Visual**
- Figure opens circuit breaker panel.
- Side view + slight grin.
- Quick alternation: face close-up -> panel close-up -> face close-up.

**SFX / Music**
- Clicks, relay ticks, metallic creaks.

### 0:22–0:32
**Visual**
- Extreme close-up of gloved hands ripping components/wires (rapid cuts).
- Short bursts of sparks (practical or VFX).
- Wire tosses out of frame.

**SFX / Music**
- Harsh electrical crackle + sharp whooshes.

### 0:32–0:38
**Visual**
- Abrupt blackout.
- Tiny emergency light flicker.

**Audio**
- Echoing laugh in darkness.

**On-screen text (impact frame)**
- `THE SYSTEM IS DOWN.`

---

## 0:38–0:50 — Reveal Transition

**Visual**
- Glitch transition from black into the Dead Circuit interface.
- Fast montage of timer, alarm module, threat index, and telemetry scope.

**Voiceover**

**On-screen text**

## Shot & Edit Notes (Practical)

- Keep first act visually dark, second act neon/energetic for contrast.
- Use quick cuts (0.5–1.5s) during sabotage sequence and gameplay highlights.
- Use whoosh transitions + glitch overlays to match UI aesthetic.
- Keep captions bold, uppercase, short.
- Target pacing: **~125–145 words voiceover total** for clear delivery in 1:50.

## Safety / Production Notes

- Use controlled props; avoid filming live electrical tampering.
- Sparks should be simulated with VFX or safe practical FX under supervision.
- Mark opening sequence as dramatized in final credits if required.
